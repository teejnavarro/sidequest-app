# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Filipino travel groups of about 2 to 10 friends planning a trip together. Many are not technical, and most use the app in a phone browser, often after tapping an invite link in a group chat. One person may be in several trips with different groups. Recruiters and interviewers also read the public code.

## Product Purpose
Sidequest lets a group plan a trip in one place (schedule, ideas with voting, flight and hotel details) and track shared costs the way Splitwise does. Success means a group plans a real trip here instead of in a shared document. It is also how the owner is learning full-stack development.

## Positioning
Built around how Filipino groups actually travel: settling up with GCash, Maya or bank transfer, and using a common fund, with shared costs handled "without the awkward chats".

## Operating Context
People plan over weeks, then rely on the app during the trip, including offline viewing with poor signal. Invites travel by shared link. Times follow the trip's own time zone.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Each trip is a separate private space. Non-members can't see it.
- Sign-in is with Google. Before sign-in, an invite shows only limited trip details and the owner's name.
- Money is exact to the centavo. Payment details and screenshots are private data.
- Budget is ₱0, on free hosting and database tiers that must not pause mid-trip.
- The code is public, so it never contains real names, trips, costs, payment details or secret keys.
- Native apps are out of scope.

## Brand Commitments
The name is Sidequest. The voice is short, plain and friendly, as in "without the awkward chats". It has its own design and doesn't copy Splitwise. The approved visual direction lives in `docs/design.md`.

## Evidence on Hand
No testimonials, customers or usage numbers exist yet. Don't invent any.

## Product Principles
- Easy for a non-technical friend to use on the first try.
- Screens should feel specific to friends planning a trip, not like a generic app.
- Small details in wording and visuals are part of the work.
- Money is exact and clear, and private data stays private.
- Works when it's needed most: on a phone, during the trip.
